# Dungeon visual quality studies v2

Response to the user's new visual references and rejection of monotonous stone, wetness, modules and orange light. Four source-bound room paintovers establish purposeful variation: cold/dry upper crossing; pale mineral well descent; quiet matte red ritual plaster; cold drowned choir. The layout/camera is the invariant; local materials, construction finish, water and damage are proposed art direction.

Two original square albedo textures study distinct material families: pale lime plaster/limestone and dark layered schist. They are new material sources for later blending, not a promise that repeating one map across an entire room will create variation. A posed creature image supplies a sculpt/rig reference; it is not a 3D model or game-ready mesh.

Each output has an exact prompt. `brief.json` records all input roles and sources; `generation.json` and `SHA256SUMS` record the selected native outputs and review. No runtime assets or gameplay are changed by this alignment pass. All files inherit the dungeon art directory's `.gdignore` and are excluded from game import/export.

Review: four environment outputs are 1586 × 992; two diffuse material sources are 1254 × 1254 native outputs despite the 2048 request; the clothed watcher reference is 1024 × 1536. No upscale was applied. Textures were inspected and their opposite edges compared numerically; they remain candidate material sources requiring an actual tiling/PBR test. Do not apply one combined damaged-wall map indiscriminately: use separate base materials, localized blend masks, varied geometry and restrained object-specific wear.

The watcher is a nonsexual adult in layered covering clothes. The first attempt produced no image because the tool rejected its output; the final clothing-focused prompt succeeded. The archived first prompt and failure receipt are in `brief.json` for traceability. No failed output is shipped.

All seven selected outputs were visually reviewed. Room views preserve primary framing and structure, with local generated drift recorded per asset. The drowned choir invents a low central stone enclosure; do not silently adopt it into the gameplay layout. These are proposals for alignment, not proof of implemented graphics or acceptance of the existing structural geometry.

The newly attached source photos/illustrations were supplied by the user as visual references. Their underlying provenance/licensing is unverified; source files are not bundled as game assets. Only original generated studies and provenance metadata are included here. Existing SECHA screenshot/reference provenance remains with its original project assets.
