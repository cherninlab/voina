# Red Descent — first dungeon maps

Proposed layout for the visual direction selected in this conversation. This is one dungeon with three connected floors, two timber crossings, and a shared shaft. The maps are ready for review; they do not replace the current runtime dungeon.

| Map | Elevation | Main purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `00-cutaway.png` | All floors | Atmospheric overview of the three levels and their relationship |
| `01-upper-floor.png` / `.svg` | 0 m | Arrival from the forest, first exposed crossing, optional restraint cell, S1 descent |
| `02-middle-floor.png` / `.svg` | −6 m | Bent low tunnel, ceiling threat, lower crossing, distant witness, S2 descent |
| `03-lower-floor.png` / `.svg` | −12 m | Shaft-base chamber, small ember hearth, side cells and a perimeter return loop |

The route starts at the north arrival stair, crosses west to east, descends S1 in the southeast, circles through the low western tunnel, crosses east again, follows the witness passage north, then descends S2 in the northwest. At the base, the west and east passages form a loop around the hearth chamber. Return through S2, S1 and the arrival stair. No lift, locked gate or new travel mechanic is required by this layout.

## Reading the plans

Gold marks the suggested route, coral diamonds mark encounter or sightline positions, and dashed blue marks the lower return loop. S1 and S2 identify paired stairwells with identical footprints on the two floors they connect. The grid is 2 m; each map has a 10 m scale bar and the same north orientation. Floor heights are relative to the upper arrival level.

`layout.json` records metre coordinates, room footprints, partition segments, routes and nominal clearances. The overall envelope is 36 × 44 m. The shaft and central bottom chamber measure 12 × 16 m. Both bridges are 3 m wide; the narrowest intended tunnel is 4 m wide. Ordinary enclosed ceilings target 3.4 m, with at least 2.6 m clear below the ceiling encounter. Dogleg stair footprints reserve two 3 m flights and 3 m landings; tread symbols in the plans are schematic. Actual stair surfaces, ceiling geometry, camera behavior and sword clearance need validation in the Godot blockout.

The three creature positions are encounter concepts, not existing assets or implemented behavior. Keep the restraint alcove optional and the ceiling threat clear of the floor route. The bottom is deliberately wider to let players move and fight after the enclosed passages.

## Source and checks

The cutaway was generated with built-in Codex ImageGen using the selected red-descent scene, restraint-cell image and floor-plan drafts as references. A targeted second pass moved the lower stair to the opposite side. Both exact prompts are included. The cutaway communicates atmosphere and vertical organization; use the SVG plans and JSON for coordinates, since generated arches, stair shapes and proportions remain illustrative.

The floor plans were drawn deterministically as editable SVGs and matching PNGs. Their text and dimensions were not generated by ImageGen. The PNGs were visually inspected; static checks confirmed connected quarter-metre floor cells, routes inside floor footprints and away from the recorded partitions, matching stair footprints, 6 m drops between floors, PNG integrity and valid SVG XML. `generation.json` records provenance and check results; `SHA256SUMS` records output image hashes.

All files remain under the dungeon art-direction `.gdignore`. No game code changed and no runtime acceptance is implied. Camera collision issue T-129 remains separate.
