## Lookout

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: VALKA game visual development comparison, NOT an actual game screenshot.
Input image 1 is the current game capture and strict composition reference. Input image 2 is the desired rustic realism, terrain, palette and atmosphere reference.
Re-render image 1 at the environmental quality of Enshrouded and The Witcher, with the grounded materials and atmospheric depth of Death Stranding. Preserve the exact camera angle, framing, major building positions, foreground red market canopy, well on left, center-right wooden lookout with pitched roof and its ramp, palisade behind, and open routes. Do not turn this into an unrelated grand city. Correct structural rafters so they lean inward to support roof ridges, all posts physically meet beams. Replace primitive material appearance with credible weathered timber, individual irregular thatch, rough stone, textile red cloth. Replace perfectly uniform ground with gently undulating compacted earth, inset worn cobbles, ruts, stones, grass clumps and natural material transitions. Keep traversable routes broad and uncluttered. Replace smooth sphere hills with layered forested rocky hills. Physically grounded sunlight, soft sky bounce, contact shadows under every prop, subtle distance haze, textured clouds, natural exposure, restrained highlights. Varied forest greens, sky blue, stone gray and muted red accents. No sepia filter, no exaggerated bloom, no UI, no logos, no text. Deliver one coherent wide landscape image, respect the source composition closely.

## Elevated village

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: VALKA environment comparison concept.
Input image 1 is an actual prototype game capture: strict layout and camera reference. Input image 2 supplies the target medieval mountain village material quality, color variety and sense of inhabited place.
Rebuild image 1 visually at the quality of Enshrouded / The Witcher / Death Stranding environment art while preserving the elevated camera, irregular palisade perimeter, same small village scale, central open combat clearing, west lookout with ramp, well left of center, five asymmetrically clustered cottages, market stalls and connected routes. Keep all major landmarks in the same screen regions so the result is directly comparable. Improve only the environmental execution: terrain has small believable elevation changes, ruts, worn stone paths and compressed dirt, stones embedded into soil, patches of moss and grass that merge naturally, no sharp green puddle-shaped patches. Cottages retain rustic timber/stone/thatched architecture with correct load-bearing roof structures, varied weathering, modest red and teal cloth accents. Replace gigantic smooth sphere hills with credible forested slopes, rocky outcrops and atmospheric layered mountains. Make boundaries read as an organic settlement within a landscape rather than a board on a flat plane. Broad navigable center, peripheral prop clusters, natural vegetation density. Realistic PBR material response, soft directional sunlight and skylight, contact shadows and subtle indirect bounce, blue sky with substantial textured clouds, haze in the distance. Rich but natural green, blue, gray, warm timber, muted red and teal; no monochrome grading. No UI, no writing, no logos. One wide high-quality game environment concept; do not change to a close-up or add large castles.
