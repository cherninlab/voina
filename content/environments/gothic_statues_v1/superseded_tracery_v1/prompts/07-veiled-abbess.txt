Composition: ONE complete isolated sculpture and its attached modest integral base, all edges and base visible with 10 percent margin. One view of one object.

Construction: substantial joined forms that could be carved and reconstructed as one robust 3D object; accessories visibly connected to hands and torso; thick, plausible stone horns and wings. Distinct silhouette, meaningful asymmetry and localized wear.

Dense cascading folds and one fractured edge at the hem, low octagonal plinth, no horns, no wings. Solemn funerary sculpture with ominous ambiguity.
