## Goal
Make the physics ageing of the wooden bridges visible at play distance. The first pass (T-169, 1e8718c) moved boards 1–3 cm, which does not read in game.

Levers, all seeded and run through the existing attempt series (`scripts/art/bake_bridges.py`):
- **Missing boards:** 1–4 per bridge, rotted out.
- **Rotten posts:** they already lean 3–7° outward and can lean further in a tremor; rails and rope follow them.
- **Failed rail ends:** these rails hang.
- **Pulled nails:** where physics pulled a board's nail, that board end curls up 1.5–4 cm over its outer 35 cm, outside the walking lanes.
- **Rope:** uneven slack.
- **Damping:** heavier on rails, so attempts settle in minutes.
- **Picking:** the target is about one board in ten loose, 2 missing, 1–2 posts leaning and 1–2 rails hanging, as long as the bridge stays walkable.
- **Contact sheet:** each attempt gets a wide view and a close-up of its most damaged spot.

## Done when
- In-game frames (`scripts/capture_bridges.gd`, `scripts/capture_dungeon.gd`) show visible but believable ageing.
- The bridge contract passes: no sideways falls, and the deck is within 3 cm of the collision on the lanes, except at the listed missing boards.
- Traversal, smoke and import tests pass.
- Contact sheets and frames are saved in `review/bridge_aged_v2`.
