## Goal
Theo's paintovers (content/art_direction/theo_paintovers_v1) and his revision of the antechamber frame are far darker than the game. Light comes in warm pools from torches with strong falloff. Corners and the vault go nearly black. The abyss is filled with cold green-grey fog, and red light comes from the shaft depths through doorways. Stone is desaturated grey-brown and wet: floors reflect torchlight. Currently everything is evenly warm orange. Tune Environment (ambient, exposure, tonemap, fog, glow), torch light colour, energy, range and shadows, the abyss glow lights, and per-material wetness and tint in crypt_dungeon.gd so captured frames match the paintovers. The renderer is Mobile, so there is no SSAO, SSR or SDFGI; check what volumetric fog costs. Keep gameplay readable: route edges, bridges and enemies must stay visible, so verify with the bridge tour and room captures. Use scripts/capture_dungeon.gd (--stone-rooms, --views=) and scripts/capture_bridges.gd (--tour) through scripts/godot_bg.sh. Do not edit red_descent_geometry.gd or dungeon_surface.gdshader (T-177).

## Done when
- Side-by-side review frames (paintover vs game) in content/environments/red_descent/review/grade_v1/ show matching value structure and palette; smoke and dungeon tests pass; docs/red-descent-grade.md records the settings and why.
