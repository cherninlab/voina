## Goal
Theo's crypt references (content/art_direction/theo_paintovers_v1 and his revision of the antechamber frame) have dark blood-red roots everywhere: thick trunks that come out of joints high on walls and vault crowns, cling to the stone as they run down (never floating, never cutting through it), split into thinner veins, hang in loops across arches, and spread over the floor at the wall base. Build a reusable Blender generator (shared toolkit scripts/art/procgen, headless 5.2: blender --factory-startup -b --python-exit-code 1 --python ...). Input: the stone builds content/environments/procedural_architecture_v1/rooms/<id>.glb and passages/*.glb (from scripts/art/build_architecture.py, owned by T-177, theo-claude-14) plus the layout content/environments/procedural_bridge_v1/layout.json. Grow by gravity plus surface cling (raycast/BVH against the room meshes), tapered branching tubes with bark-like twist, a few hanging strands under arches; keep the walking ribbons (routes in layout.json, width) clear of anything above ankle height so traversal is unaffected. Output per room: content/environments/procedural_roots_v1/<id>.glb (meshes named Roots_*, vertex colour = baked AO, no collision) + JSON report; budget about 15k triangles per room. T-177 integrates them in red_descent_geometry.gd; do not edit gameplay files.

## Done when
- Roots GLBs exist for every room in procedural_architecture_v1/rooms, review renders under torch light show roots clinging to (not intersecting) the stone, and docs/procedural-generators.md describes the generator.
