## Goal
Generate broken masonry and rock chunks with CL3D (`~/Projects/CherninLab3D`) as physics sources for the rubble generator (T-163).

- Assets, 0.15–0.8 m:
  - broken ashlar blocks (6)
  - rough rocks (6)
  - broken column drums (3)
  - brick clumps (3)
- Same steps and contract as T-166, except:
  - meshes must be closed and watertight, because rigid bodies use convex hulls
  - origin at the bounding-box centre
  - LOD0 at or under 1.5k tris
  - flat-ish break faces are fine
- Same provenance rule as T-166: record CL3D as Tencent Hunyuan3D-2mini under the Tencent license.
- No game integration: T-163 consumes `manifest.json`.

## Done when
- At least 15 watertight chunks have LODs and a manifest.
- A contact sheet is in the folder.
- Godot import is clean.
