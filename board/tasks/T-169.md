## Goal
Age generated construction with physics instead of hand-placed noise. Build each piece as designed, join the pieces with joints of random strength, disturb them (tremors, a rolling stone ball, debris), run a series of seeded attempts, measure each one, and keep the most believable result that is still walkable.

- **`scripts/art/procgen/disturb.py`**
  - joints: nail, pin, hinge with lean limits
  - weight-scaled weak joints
  - `shake`, `roll`, displacements, Cycles review renders, contact-sheet PNGs
- **`scripts/art/procgen/fracture.py`**
  - Voronoi chunks with material grain: stone, brick, plaster, wood (cells stretched along the grain)
  - hairline cracks between chunks
  - breakable glue scaled to chunk weight
- **Bridges** (`build_procedural_bridge.py`): every board, post and rail is a rigid body. There are 8 attempts per route, from calm to violent, some with a ball. Each attempt is classified untouched, aged, damaged, collapsed or unwalkable. The best walkable "aged" attempt is rebuilt and exported, and the report lists missing-board footprints.
- **Parallel runs:** `scripts/art/bake_bridges.py` runs the attempts as parallel Blender processes, since Bullet uses one core.
- **Precision:** 40 substeps, 60 solver iterations, MESH collision on supports, 0.2 mm margins.

## Done when
- `selftest_disturb.py` and `selftest_fracture.py` pass: calm leaves everything untouched, disturbance loosens or breaks pieces, and wood cells run along the grain.
- Both bridges are re-baked from an "aged" attempt, with a contact sheet of all attempts in `review/bridge_aged_v1`.
- The bridge contract passes: no sideways falls, and the deck stays within 3 cm of the collision on the lanes except at the listed missing boards. Traversal, smoke and import pass, and same-camera frames are inspected.
