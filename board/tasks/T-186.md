## Goal
Theo's idea: explorer bots that find every way through the dungeon and report. The fixed traversal test (tests/dungeon_traversal_test.gd) only walks route centre lines, so it misses snags off the line (thresholds, rubble hulls, parapet ends, gaps along walls, props). It drives the real player capsule; read it for the move/slide/snap loop and the 4x time scale. Build headless explorer bots that use the same capsule physics: (1) Sample the walkable space: a 0.5 m grid of downward rays over the dungeon bounds gives floor cells with an upward normal, 2 m of headroom and a slope under 45 degrees, grouped into areas (rooms from RedDescentLayout.rooms(), passages and bridges from RedDescentLayout.routes(); index.json in content/environments/procedural_architecture_v1 has stone passage coverage). (2) Several bots explore frontier-first: they walk with physics from reached cells to neighbouring unreached ones, without teleporting except to restart. They record reached cells, cells that are walkable but unreachable, stuck events (no progress for N seconds, with the slide collisions' collider paths and positions), falls (an unplanned drop over 2.5 m, with where they landed), ledges, and narrow chokepoints. (3) Travel times: walk between every pair of adjacent areas along the best found path. (4) Write telemetry JSON plus top-down heatmaps per level (reached, unreached, issues) as PNGs, and an issues list sorted by severity with the colliding node path. These names are the builder's (Collision_<room>_<n> stone cores, StonePassage_*, Props_*, Rubble_*), so each issue says which system to fix. Provide scripts/dungeon_explorer.py "" like the other tests, plus a fast mode for CI and a full mode. Do not change gameplay or level code; report the issues found to the T-185 owner (theo-claude-14).

## Done when
- Explorer runs headless, produces telemetry JSON, heatmaps and an issues list for the current dungeon; docs/dungeon-explorer.md explains it; findings are reported on T-185.
