## Goal
Build Red Descent's architecture from real stones in Blender, then bake it (Theo, 2026-10-24: "build the whole dungeon of the actual bricks and stones ... and only after that bake it"). Every element has a structural reason:
- openings only where a route really crosses a wall;
- one generator per element: an enclosed route's doorways are the ends of its own vault, built together;
- a stone build removes every old box it replaces, and the shaft outline wall stops at rooms instead of running through them.
Target look: Theo's paintovers (content/art_direction/theo_paintovers_v1) and his crypt references, which have rough squared stone, tight dark joints, arches on piers, vaults, and wet, dark stone.

Helpers: T-178 roots, T-179 wall-base rubble, T-180 stone surface textures.

## Done when
- Every room and every flat enclosed passage is stone-built (scripts/art/build_architecture.py → content/environments/procedural_architecture_v1/, index.json), with stairs and sloped tunnels following.
- No arch without a passage, no box inside an arch, no wall through a room (checked in --stone-rooms captures).
- Traversal, structure, bridge and smoke tests pass.
- docs/dungeon-architecture.md describes the rules and the pipeline.
