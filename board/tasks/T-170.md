## Goal
Make wall, pier and arch modules for Red Descent that look aged by real physics, not by noise. Use `scripts/art/procgen/fracture.py` and `disturb.py` (T-169), and read `docs/procedural-generators.md` "Physics ageing" first.

1. **Build:** model ashlar walls, piers and arches as individual blocks with mortar joints. Glue the blocks with `fracture.glue`: brick or stone strength, and weaker for old mortar.
2. **Fracture:** split a few blocks with Voronoi (`fracture.voronoi`, material `stone`) so chunks can spall off.
3. **Disturb:** run tremor and impact attempts (`disturb.shake` on the footing, `disturb.roll` stones) over several seeds and intensities.
4. **Classify** each attempt: intact, weathered (a few stones slipped or missing, edges chipped), cracked, or collapsed (a breach, with rubble at the foot). Export one or two of each useful class.
5. **Output per module:**
   - a GLB at metre scale, with AO in Color.R and fresh break faces in Color.B
   - convex collision for fallen chunks
   - a report with seed, attempt metrics and sha256
6. **Review:** a contact sheet of all attempts, plus an in-engine preview capture.
7. **Placement:** don't place modules in Red Descent. Note anchor suggestions on T-162.

## Done when
- At least 3 module types (wall bay, pier, arch) have 3 condition classes each.
- Calm attempts come out untouched.
- No floating stones (gap ≤ 1 cm) and no interpenetration over 1 cm.
- Import and smoke tests pass.
