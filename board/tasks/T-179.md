## Goal
Theo's revision of the antechamber frame piles broken stones and gravel along wall bases and in corners, as fallen from the walls. Using the toolkit (scripts/art/procgen: fracture, rigid, soil, stones, disturb), drop fractured stone pieces and gravel with Blender rigid-body physics against the stone builds content/environments/procedural_architecture_v1/rooms/<id>.glb (built by scripts/art/build_architecture.py, owned by T-177 theo-claude-14) and the floor at room['floor'] from content/environments/procedural_bridge_v1/layout.json, mostly hugging wall bases and corners, denser under arches and old cracks, a few larger blocks. Keep the walking ribbons (layout routes, width + 0.3 m) clear above 4 cm so traversal and the bridge contract hold. Bake the settled result per room to content/environments/procedural_rubble_v1/<id>.glb (meshes Rubble_*, vertex colour = AO times tone, small convex collision only for pieces over 25 cm, named -convcolonly) plus a JSON report; budget about 20k triangles per room. T-177 integrates them in red_descent_geometry.gd; do not edit gameplay files.

## Done when
- Rubble GLBs exist for every room in procedural_architecture_v1/rooms, review renders show settled, plausible piles (no floating or intersecting pieces), routes stay clear, and docs/procedural-generators.md describes the generator.
