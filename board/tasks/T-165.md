## Goal
Replace the flat, tiled room floors with real stone floor geometry made in Blender.

- Generate seamless 4×4 m floor modules, plus edge and border strips, of irregular flagstones:
  - varied stone sizes with 6–15 mm joints
  - ±1–2 cm height and tilt variation
  - chipped edges, and some cracked or missing stones with dirt fill
- Bevel the stones and bake AO into vertex colour. Use UVs at real-world scale and agree texel density with the materials owner (T-161).
- Optional: generate floors per room polygon from the layout JSON written by T-159's `scripts/art/export_dungeon_layout.gd`.
- Visual only: walking collision stays flat. Keep the variation at 2 cm or less so feet don't visibly sink or float.
- Output a GLB with LODs and a report JSON (seed, tris, sha256, relative paths). Blender 5.2.2 with `--factory-startup`.
- Don't place floors in Red Descent; T-162 integrates.

## Done when
- At least 3 module variants tile without visible seams in a 3×3 test grid.
- LOD0 stays at or under 20k tris per 4×4 m module.
- Godot import is clean.
- A Blender render and an in-engine capture under torch light are saved in `review/flagstone_v1/`.
