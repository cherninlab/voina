## Goal
Replace the rejected wooden bridge deck on `high_span` and `low_span` (planks, stringers, chain rails) with bridges baked in Blender from the real route data, and build the shared Blender toolkit the other generator tasks reuse.

- `scripts/art/export_dungeon_layout.gd` (headless Godot) writes routes, rooms and mitered edges to JSON.
- `blender --factory-startup -b --python-exit-code 1 --python scripts/art/build_procedural_bridge.py -- <layout.json> <outdir>` builds each span:
  - varied planks laid across the walking direction
  - continuous stringers and real joints at bends
  - posts, iron straps, and hanging chain rails with real-size links and ring anchors
  - AO baked into vertex colour
  - one GLB per span, plus a report JSON (layout hash, seed, sha256)
- `dungeon_structure.gd` loads the GLBs instead of building deck and rail timbers. Supports, piers, the roof and the other `timber()` callers stay.
- Walking collision stays in `RedDescentGeometry`, with an unchanged contract.
- Shared toolkit `scripts/art/procgen/` (Blender Python): seeded RNG, timber and plank builders, catenary chain and rope, AO bake, GLB export, report writer. T-163, T-164 and T-165 import it. Ask for changes with a note on this task.
- Thin iron parts (rings, links, straps, bolts) are procedural here. Chunky optional parts come from T-166's manifest when available.

## Done when
- Both spans render with no stair-stepping and no plank overlap or z-fight at bends. Plank ends stop inside posts and landings, and chains are attached at both ends. Same-camera before/after frames are saved in `review/bridge_v1/`.
- `scripts/bridge_construction_test.gd` passes:
  - the deck top is within 3 cm of the collision ribbon on the walk lanes
  - no member floats
  - the layout hash matches, so a stale bake fails
  - the suspected guard-collision gap on the outside of bends is measured (about 0.9 m at `high_span` node 2, by calculation; the player capsule is 0.8 m). If it is real, it is reported to T-162 with numbers.
- Import, main smoke, dungeon smoke, `scripts/dungeon_structure_test.gd` and the 1,276 m traversal pass.
- `docs/procedural-generators.md` explains the pipeline to other agents.
