## Goal
Make dressing sets for Red Descent rooms by placing props "as they were left", then knocking them about with physics (T-169's `disturb.py`).

1. **Props** are rigid bodies:
   - tables, benches, coffins with sliding lids, shelves with pots and bones, barrels and candles
   - procedural with `scripts/art/procgen`, or chunky CL3D parts if T-166 or T-167 manifests exist
2. **Breakables:** pots and planks can break with `fracture.py` (plaster or wood materials).
3. **Disturb:** run tremors, a rolling stone ball through the set, and things falling off shelves. Run a series of seeds and intensities and keep the believable ones. Mix lightly disturbed and wrecked sets.
4. **Output per set:**
   - a GLB, with convex collision for large props only
   - a footprint, and a lane-clearance check so nothing blocks a doorway or route corridor
   - a report with seed, attempt metrics and sha256
5. **Review:** a contact sheet and an in-engine capture.
6. **Placement:** don't place sets in Red Descent. Note suggestions on T-162.

## Done when
- At least 4 set types, each with 3 variants.
- A calm attempt leaves its set untouched.
- No floating props and nothing intersecting.
- Import and smoke tests pass.
