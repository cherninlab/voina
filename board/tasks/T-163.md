## Goal
Make a Blender generator that drops stones and broken planks under rigid-body physics, lets them settle, and bakes the result into static rubble piles for the dungeon.

- Run headless: `blender --factory-startup -b --python-exit-code 1 --python scripts/art/build_rubble.py -- <outdir> [--seed N]`, with Blender 5.2.2.
- Reuse `scripts/art/procgen/` from T-159 for RNG, AO bake, export and the report once it ships. Until then keep helpers local and move them later.
- Stone sources:
  - procedural by default: fractured or displaced ashlar fragments, brick clumps and rough rocks, 0.1–0.8 m
  - optional: CL3D chunks listed in T-167's `manifest.json`
- Recipes, 3 seeds each:
  - wall-base spill (against a vertical plane)
  - corner heap
  - collapsed-bridge debris (stones plus broken planks)
  - loose floor scatter
- Per pile:
  - settle until the maximum speed is under 1 cm/s
  - apply transforms and merge
  - bake AO into vertex colour
  - make 3 LODs
  - export a GLB with Godot collision: `-convcolonly` hulls for big stones, or one simplified `-colonly` mesh
  - put the origin at the ground centre and record the footprint in metres
- Report JSON: seed, frames, residual speed, maximum interpenetration, tris per LOD, sha256, relative paths only.
- Don't place piles in Red Descent: `crypt_dungeon.gd` belongs to integration (T-162). Suggest anchor spots in a note on T-162.

## Done when
- At least 4 recipes × 3 seeds are baked.
- No stone floats more than 1 cm above its support or interpenetrates more than 1 cm.
- Godot import is clean.
- Blender turntable renders and one in-engine capture are saved in `review/rubble_v1/`.
- A test capsule can stand on a pile.
