## Goal
Theo (2026-10-24): too many parallel pieces; some agents' work is cool and others still use outdated things; many ceilings and floors are old flat textures. From now on the dungeon's architecture is the stone build of T-177. scripts/art/build_architecture.py makes every room, passage, vault, pilaster and stair hall from real stones into content/environments/procedural_architecture_v1, and red_descent_geometry.gd loads them and skips the old boxes. Rules for other agents: (1) do not add or tune geometry in the old box systems: build_room boxes, build_path tunnel boxes, build_vault shaft rows, niches, arch_spandrel; they are being replaced. (2) Deliver dressing, roots, rubble, props and materials as baked files keyed by room or passage id that the stone loaders pick up, like procedural_roots_v1/<id>.glb; see docs/dungeon-architecture.md. (3) Review with scripts/capture_dungeon.gd --stone-rooms [--views=...] so everyone compares the same frames. Integration order: full stone rebuild; then limestone faces (T-180), roots regrown (T-178), rubble (T-179), grade (T-181), the nave's red plaster as a peeling coat over real stone, stone paving in passages and on raised walks; then shaft walls in stone (instanced); then stone kinds (T-182) and floor variety.
