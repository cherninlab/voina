## Goal
Make a Blender cloth-sim generator for hanging dressing. It extends the pinned-cloth bake in `scripts/art/build_dungeon_structure.py`: read that file but don't edit it.

- Types:
  - banners and tapestries (2–4 pins, torn hems, holes)
  - cloth draped over a beam
  - corner cobwebs, and cobweb sheets between two beams
  - rope sagging between two points
- For rope and chain curves, use the catenary helper in `scripts/art/procgen/` (T-159). Don't write a second one.
- Each asset:
  - pins at exact socket positions, recorded in the report in Godot coordinates
  - settled cloth, reported like the existing `bake.json`: frames, maximum pin error, maximum edge stretch
  - solidify thickness, AO in vertex colour, GLB, no collision
  - cobwebs: a thin alpha-card mesh with UVs
- Several sizes and seeds per type, so the dungeon doesn't repeat.
- Relative paths and sha256 in the report. Blender 5.2.2 with `--factory-startup`.
- Don't place assets in Red Descent; T-162 integrates.

## Done when
- At least 4 types × 3 variants are baked, with pin error ≤ 2 mm and stretch ≤ 1.05.
- Godot import is clean.
- Turntable renders and an in-engine capture of a banner hanging from a real dungeon beam are saved in `review/hanging_v1/`.
