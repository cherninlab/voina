## Goal
Generate chunky bridge furniture with the local CL3D pipeline (`~/Projects/CherninLab3D`). These are optional parts for the procedural bridge generator (T-159).

Don't make thin parts here (rings, chain, straps, bolts): image-to-3D loses thin shapes, so T-159 builds those procedurally.

- Assets, 2–3 variants each:
  - stone corbel carrying a beam end, about 0.5×0.4×0.6 m
  - forged iron knee bracket, about 0.6 m
  - carved wooden post finial for a 0.18 m post
  - wall anchor plate with a thick ring, about 0.25 m
  - iron-shod post foot
- Steps per asset:
  1. Make a reference image: a single object on a plain background, 3/4 view.
  2. Generate the shape with CL3D 2mini and record the seed.
  3. Clean it with `scripts/art/prepare_dungeon_kit.py`: islands, normals, scale to real metres.
  4. Make 3 LODs, with LOD0 at or under 3k tris.
- Contract:
  - one GLB per asset, in metres, Y up
  - origin at the mount point: the wall or post contact face at z = 0, with the object extending toward +Z; finials and feet sit on the post axis
  - `manifest.json` lists id, kind, dims_m, tris per LOD, origin rule, seed, prompt, input/raw/output sha256 and timings
  - relative paths only; T-156's `generation.json` had absolute paths into another worktree
- Provenance in `README.md`: CL3D is a local build of Tencent Hunyuan3D-2mini, under the Tencent Hunyuan 3D 2.0 Community License, which does not apply in the EU, UK or South Korea. Record it that way; release use is the owner's decision (see T-118).
- No game integration: T-159 consumes the manifest.

## Done when
- At least 10 assets are cleaned, with LODs and a manifest.
- A Blender turntable contact sheet is in the folder.
- Godot import is clean.
