## Goal
Mike wants the village cultists' robes (content/mobs/hooded_cultist, T-107/T-109) to move like heavy linen with real weight and simulation, above all when a cultist is cut in half.

Today the gown, sleeves and hood are plain skinned meshes on the 65-bone skeleton: the gown is a stiff bell weighted mostly to the pelvis (hem follows the thighs at most 30 %), and the legs and feet under it are deleted (`LEG_HIDE` in scripts/art/build_hooded_cultist.py) so a jogging leg never clips through. A bisect bakes the plane into the garment like any skinned mesh (MobRagdoll.bake_cut), so each half is a rigid hollow shell of robe on its ragdoll.

Direction (pick and justify; no external plugins, Godot 4.7.2 Mobile/Metal, built-in Jolt):
- Hem and sleeves with weight while alive: e.g. a few spring-driven skirt bones or a light verlet/PBD cloth on the hem ring, driven by the body's velocity, gravity and leg swing, colliding with capsules on thighs/calves so it stays outside the (hidden) legs.
- When cut: the robe should fall and drape like cloth, not stay a rigid shell: e.g. the cut garment becomes a SoftBody3D or a PBD cloth pinned to the half's ragdoll parts, sagging, folding onto the ground and terrain, with the cut edge free and frayed. Consider the lower half (an empty robe) collapsing onto the ground.
- Keep it cheap: up to 5 cultists alive plus a few corpses; measure frame cost (see T-111 perf meter) and cap simulated corpses.

## Done when
- Live robes sway with weight when walking, jogging, turning, jabbing and getting hit, without legs or arms clipping through; no visible stretching tears.
- Cut robes on both halves fall and drape as cloth onto the ground within ~1–2 s, the cut edge loose; ragdolls, blood, caps and halves still work; nothing jitters or explodes.
- A headless test covers the simulation staying finite and settling, and the cut path; scripts/mob_smoke_test.py runs it.
- Captures (side view mid-jog, a hit, a bisect falling) and a measured frame cost with 5 live cultists and 2 cut ones on this Mac; docs/hooded-cultist.md updated.
- Mike playtests it before it ships.
